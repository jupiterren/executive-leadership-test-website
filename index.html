Leadership Accelerators

Targeted, practical capability-building for public sector leaders — designed to deliver real impact fast, without stepping away from the work that matters.

Browse All Programs Download 2026 Program Guide

ANZSOG's Leadership Accelerators are targeted offerings that deliver rapid, practical capability-building for public servants at every career stage — from system thinking and policy design to regulatory practice and data for public value. Whether you're a new manager stepping up or a senior leader navigating exponential change, there's a program designed for where you are right now.

100% Public sector focused
Our Offering

Built for the realities of government

Leadership Accelerators bring together the best of ANZSOG's academic rigour and practitioner insight in short, high-impact formats. Programs are available online, in-person and blended — designed to fit around the demands of busy public sector roles.

Three distinct streams give you flexibility to build capability exactly where you need it most.

Program Streams

Public Leadership Credentials

Structured, stackable credentials that build recognised capability in core areas of public leadership — from policy acumen and regulatory practice to data, digital and fiscal resilience. Ideal for mid-career professionals seeking formal recognition of their professional development.

Online Self-paced Certificate on completion

Leadership Intensives

Immersive, cohort-based programs that go deep on the complex challenges facing today's public sector leaders — from leading exponential change to influence, negotiation and transforming government organisations. Delivered in short, concentrated bursts to minimise time away from work.

Blended Cohort learning 2–5 days

Professional Learning Series

A suite of targeted online short courses covering the issues most relevant to contemporary public servants — from AI tools and ethical practice to working with First Nations communities, data for public value and developing policy acumen. New topics added regularly in response to emerging government priorities.

Online Flexible enrolment From 1 day
